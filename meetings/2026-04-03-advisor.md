## 3) Issues / Blockers

- Parth Gala: DDPG training on HPC was interrupted once by a node preemption (job killed after 6 hours at 600 episodes); SB3 checkpoint saving enabled but the checkpoint interval was set too infrequently (every 200 episodes); re-configuring to checkpoint every 50 episodes before next run.

- Rodrigo Chen: The orchestrator service design is not yet finalized; need to decide between a polling-based architecture (orchestrator queries DB every N seconds) vs. an event-driven architecture (orchestrator reacts to new price record events via a message queue); team leans toward event-driven but this adds complexity.
